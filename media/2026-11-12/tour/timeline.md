# Timeline of the tour, 2026-11-12

One row per act. `mm:ss` is the start of the act in the MP4 of its chapter; `joined` is the same moment in `tour-all.mp4`. Stills are in `stills/` (two files `-L` and `-R` for the two-screen chapter 7). Build: claude/tour at 39539ed (cargo build, debug). Nothing is cut; each act is preceded and followed by a pause of about one second.

## Method and pacing

Recorded with `choractl record` (30 fps, 1280x720, `--pointer arrow`, stepped clock) in headless sessions with the software renderer. Pointer moves take 1 s, there is a pause of about 1 s before and after each act, typing runs at 6 characters a second, content comes from `demo/content.py`. MP4s are H.264 CRF 28. Chapter 7 shows two screens side by side (each scaled to 640x360 inside 1280x720, letterboxed); the stills of that chapter are full size, one file per screen (`-L` left screen, `-R` right screen). Chapter 7 uses my own recorder with the same requests as `choractl record`, because `choractl record` takes one output (`tools/dual.py`). Chapter 7b repeats the give by menu, because the first take of that act failed (see 07-12 and 07-13). Chapter 3 is recorded in the light and in the dark theme. `tour-all.mp4` joins the chapters in this order: 1, 2, 3 light, 3 dark, 4, 5, 6, 7, 7b, 8.
Camera glides and `construct pane.manager`, `chat.show` and the voice events are method calls or events that stand in for walking, for the voice agent and for the manager panes; their rows say so.
Scripts: `tools/` (launcher, recorder, one script per chapter, `assemble.py`).
