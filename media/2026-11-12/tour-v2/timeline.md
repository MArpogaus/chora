# Timeline of tour-v2, 2026-11-12

One row per act. `mm:ss` is the start of the act in the MP4 of its chapter; `joined` is the same moment in `tour-all.mp4`. Stills are in `stills/` (two files `-L` and `-R` for the two-screen chapter 7). Build: 06044cf (the merge of the construct HUD rework), cargo build, debug. Each MP4 starts with a 2.5 s chapter slide. Nothing is cut; each act is preceded and followed by a pause of about one second.

## Method and pacing

Recorded with `choractl record` (30 fps, 1280x720, `--pointer arrow`, stepped clock) in headless sessions with the software renderer, on 06044cf. Faster than the first tour: pointer moves take about 0.55 s, there is a pause of about 0.5 s before and after each act, typing runs at 9 characters a second. Each chapter MP4 starts with a 2.5 s chapter slide; the times below include it. The flows are more natural than in the first tour: real shells with `ls` and `cat friday.md` in xp, tiling, the mc drawer and the construct; a window drag in xp; windows closed and sent to a tag in tiling; Super+t in the construct; the helper chat picked by keys. Chapter 3 is recorded in the light theme and, shorter, in the dark one. Chapter 7 shows two screens side by side (each scaled to 640x360 inside 1280x720); its stills are full size, one file per screen (`-L` left, `-R` right), recorded with my own recorder that sends the same requests as `choractl record` (`tools/dual.py`), since `choractl record` takes one output. `tour-all.mp4` joins the chapters in this order: 1, 2, 3 light, 3 dark, 4, 5, 6, 7, 8.
Camera glides, `construct pane.manager`, `chat.show` and the voice events are method calls or events that stand in for walking, for the manager panes and for the voice agent; their rows say so.
The first take of chapter 4 was redone: the key overlay keeps its search field open after one Escape, so typing went into the search. Scripts: `tools/`.
