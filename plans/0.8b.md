## Decisions of Marcel on the questions of 2026-10-24

- **The line budget:** the limit of 300 changed lines does not apply to T1
  and T3 of this plan. It still applies to T2, T4, T5 and T6. `Cargo.lock`
  lines never count.
- **T4 of 0.8:** the split into `8bfb8ce` and `4e1a1d1` stays.
- **T12 of 0.8:** `1d4d82b` stays as it is.
- **The text node:**
  - The core lays out and rasterizes text.
  - A text node reports its layout size in queries (`rects`), like an
    image without a size reports the size of its file.
  - `max_width` only wraps.
  - The text re-rasters at the physical scale of the output.
  - The text node uses the existing `color` prop; allow `color` for
    `text` in `check_kinds`.
- **Fonts:** test fonts do not go into the repo. Tests find fonts through
  the font database of cosmic-text: the system fonts plus every folder of
  the variable `CHORA_FONT_DIRS` (colon separated). The gate scripts set
  `CHORA_FONT_DIRS=/home/agent/.local/share/fonts/chora-test`. CI later
  installs `fonts-noto-cjk` and `fonts-noto-color-emoji`. A test whose
  fonts are missing answers `SKIP`, never `FAIL`.
- **T11 of 0.8:** the proposal is approved. See T5 below.
