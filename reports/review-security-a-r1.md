A client with the inject grant sends `{"key":"Tab"}`, `{"key":"Down"}`, `{"key":"Return"}`. The list opens (input.rs:228) and `choose(sel)` flies to the row, takes a window of another client as a pane, or gives a window away in the give menu, all without a person. Earliest gate: compile time. Add `injected` to NodeKey; an exhaustive destructure then forces each handler to decide. Otherwise a unit test of list_input with an injected Return.
2. MAJOR plugins/chora-mc/src/main.rs:646 and 3115 (MC-03 alias). `panel.set` is unprivileged and `set_panel` takes any panel of any scene. `{"scene":"agents","panel":"agent-1","title":"…","lines":[…]}` rewrites the agent column: its task, plan, question and last words. Those are the "words that the page show as the agent's" that MC-03 made hud.set and hud.say privileged for. The text stays until the next agent.set. The `overview` panel is open the same way. Earliest gate: unit test. Make set_panel refuse the `agents` and `overview` scenes (as OWN_TEXTS does), or make panel.set privileged.

An injected press on a window also focuses it. Earliest gate: unit test as in an_injected_press_on_a_hit_does_nothing.
