Facts that drive the three decisions:
- In r6, r7 and r8 the ricer did not open the Apps list or the key list. The only door it read without acting was the bar, and to the ricer the bar and taskbar looked like one desktop. The doors that exist today (Apps rows "Move window to the xp desktop", key list group "Other desktops: xp, media center, construct", Super+Alt+2..4) all sit behind a list that this persona never opens. So option (b) (Apps rows) and option (c) (the key list's first rows, which already name the other desktops) cannot reach it.
- The word "look" is on screen in tiling only as the colour theme: the Apps rows "Dark look", "Light look" and "System look". "Dark look" is also the preselected first row (rpt1-6). Typing the goal word "look" into Apps finds the theme rows, not the door. docs/glossary.md defines a Look as "one of tiling, xp, mc and construct".
- Every unbound Super, Super+Shift and Super+Ctrl chord is grabbed and dropped without a sign (`grab_swallowed`, main.rs:687; `Own::Swallow => return None`, wm.rs:457). The construct already handles the same case: "The keys walk here. {key} shows them all." in its HUD, at most once in 10 s (configuration.md:972, construct input.rs `keys_walk`).
- The persona tiling session does not start chora-notify (session.sh:245-250; the real flavor does, through chora-tiling-notify.service). A hint sent as a notice would be invisible to a persona and would show as rpt1-4's light card. The chip that tiling draws itself (`show_chip`, overlay.rs:175, 700 ms) needs no other process.
- The helper row is placed last on purpose (`helper_last`, fa3e20a6). fuzzel.ini shows `lines=9`, so with nine or more programs the row is off screen. The flavor rows (theme, settings, give) are sorted in among the programs.

### 1. ti-55: the door to xp, mc and construct. Decision: a "Looks" word in the bar

**Recommended change:** add a bar segment **`Looks`** right after `Apps` (modules-left: Apps, Looks, tags, layout). Like Apps and Keys, it is a fixed word with no tooltip. A click opens a fuzzel menu with the prompt **`Move this window to `** and one row for each other look that is running, in the words that the Apps rows and the key list already use: **`Move window to the xp desktop`**, **`Move window to the media center desktop`**, **`Move window to the construct desktop`**. A running look is a client name in `clients.list`, which is the same rule as xp's Send To (context.rs:429). Choosing a row runs the existing give path (`give.sh`, with the "Window not moved" notice on a refusal). If no other look is running, the menu has one row, **`No other look is running`**, and Enter or Escape closes it. In German: `Looks`, `Dieses Fenster verschieben nach `, `Kein anderer Look läuft`.
In the same change, rename the theme rows so that "look" on screen means only the four looks: `Dark look` becomes **`Dark theme`**, `Light look` becomes **`Light theme`**, and `System look` becomes **`System theme`** (de: `Dunkles Design`, `Helles Design`, `Design des Systems`).

**Why the r6..r8 persona finds it:** this persona reads the bar and matches the goal's words to the words on screen; it opens nothing else. With `Looks` in the bar, the goal word "look" is on screen, next to the Apps word it already uses (beginner T1, ricer L2). The menu that opens names xp, and it is the only list where xp is the desktop "with a start button". Option (b) and option (c) both need a list that the persona never opened in three rounds.

**Files:** flavors/tiling/waybar/config.jsonc (module `custom/looks` plus its entry in modules-left, about 8 lines; on-click `choractl run -- chora-tiling --launch looks`); plugins/chora-tiling/src/main.rs `launch` (a `looks` branch: `clients.list` on `launch_conn`, rows from `chora_kit::give::move_words`, fuzzel `--dmenu --prompt` through the flavor line, then `sh $CHORA_FLAVOR_DIR/give.sh NAME`; about 40 lines plus a unit test for the rows, about 25 lines); crates/chora-kit/src/words.rs (2 words, en and de, about 6 lines); flavors/tiling/applications/chora-look-{dark,light,system}.desktop (Name and Name[de], 6 lines); the theme row strings in crates/chora-kit/src/launcher.rs tests (4 lines), plugins/chora-tiling/src/theme.rs:1054-1055 (2 lines) and the fail messages in tests/gate-tiling-theme.sh (5 lines); docs/configuration.md (the bar list and the Apps rows, about 4 lines). **Size: about 100 lines, half of them tests.**

### 2. ti-56: a guessed Super chord. Decision: a rate-limited hint on an unbound chord; Super+q stays unbound

**Recommended change:** when an unbound Super chord is pressed (`Own::Swallow`), the tiling chip shows **`Super+Q does nothing here. Super+F1 shows all keys.`** for 3 s, at most once every 10 s, and never while the key list is open. The chord is written as the key list writes it (keysheet: `Super+Q`, `Super+Shift+X`). `Super+F1` is the first key that is bound to `keys`, found as the construct finds it. In German: **`Super+Q ist hier nicht belegt. Super+F1 zeigt alle Tasten.`** The key is still swallowed, so nothing reaches the client and M5 holds.

**ti-12 / ti-28 (awesome keymap by design): the decision still holds.**
- Binding Super+q to close costs 1 yaml line, 1 key list row and 1 doc line. It fixes only one of Kemal's guesses: r7 and r8 also tried Super+Right, Super+l, Super+Left and Super+x/w. It is a destructive guess for people coming from other window managers: in Hyprland's default config, Super+Q starts a terminal. It also breaks the awesome keymap that the key list teaches.
- The hint costs about 60 lines. It answers every guess, closes nothing, matches the construct (POLS across looks) and points to the one list that answers all of them. A ricer who wants Super+q can still write `MOD+q: close`, because the chord is free.

**Files:** plugins/chora-tiling/src/main.rs `act` (if the action is `Own::Swallow`, call `self.hint_unbound(binding)` before `turn`; about 6 lines); plugins/chora-tiling/src/overlay.rs (generalise `show_chip(word, time)` and keep the layout chip at 700 ms; add `hint_unbound` with a last-shown `Instant` and the 10 s gate; about 30 lines); crates/chora-kit/src/words.rs (a `KeyUnbound` word "{chord} does nothing here. {key} shows all keys.", en and de, about 6 lines); docs/configuration.md (one sentence beside the swallow rule). **Size: about 60 lines plus tests (about 40).**

**Red test:** (a) unit tests in main.rs on FakeCore. `act("Super+q")` puts the chip node in the scene with text `Super+Q does nothing here. Super+F1 shows all keys.`. A second `act("Super+x")` within 10 s adds no chip. `act("Super+x")` while the key list is open adds no chip. ### 3. ti-50 and rpt1-6: the order of the Apps rows. Decision: helper first, programs, then the flavor's setting and give rows

**Recommended change:** the Apps list of tiling shows **`Chat with a helper (atlas)`** (or `Chat with a helper`) as row 1, then the installed programs sorted, then the flavor's own rows (`Dark theme`, `Edit settings`, `Light theme`, `Move window to the … desktop`, `System theme`) last, each group sorted as before.
