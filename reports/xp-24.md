### Run box row
- Red: d7f8ffb5 `cargo test -p chora-xp the_row_of_the_run_box_is_28_px` (unit test on the generated ini: line-height in pt x 96/72 = 28 px), and the PM red `FAIL: 1x: the row of the Run box is 37.0 px high, not 28` (reproduced here, 5bd1bdfd).
- Cause: fuzzel 1.11 reads `line-height` in points, the pads in pixels. 28 pt x 4/3 = 37.33 px. Measured with the gate cell, row px by line-height: 16 -> 21, 20 -> 27, 24 -> 32, 28 -> 37, 32 -> 43 (inner-pad and vertical-pad do not change the row). `28px` is read as physical pixels and does not scale: 14 logical = 28 phys at 2x, so it is wrong at 2x. `horizontal-pad=12px` is not accepted (fuzzel falls back to 40).
- Fix: db4fe146 flavors/xp/theme/fuzzel.ini `line-height=21` (21 pt = 28 px at 1x, 56 phys at 2x). The static grid check in the gate no longer lists line-height (it is in pt; the rendered row is asserted).
- Cell: xp-fields, runbox 1x and 2x: row 28.0 ok at both (gate output with RUNBOX_INI probes).
- Captures: reports/xp-24/r2-runbox-1x-row28.png, r2-runbox-2x-row28.png (lh=21, hpad 13 and 13, probe runs).

### Run box text inset: open, needs a PM decision
The gate asserts that the first dark pixel of "Open:" is a multiple of 4 logical px from the inner border (tests/xp-runbox-rows.py, which I did not loosen). With `horizontal-pad=P`:

| scale | P=11 | P=12 | P=13 | P=14 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1x | - | 11 | 12 | 13 |
| 2x | 11 | 12 | 13 | - |

At 1x the ink of the "O" is P-1, at 2x it is P: the glyph's left bearing is hinted to 1 px at 14 px and is 3.5 phys px (1.75 logical) at 28 px. No single integer pad is on the grid at both scales (1x needs 13, 2x needs 12). Tried without effect: `dpi-aware=no`, `hinting=false` in the font. The font is Inter (Tahoma and DejaVu Sans are not installed here), so the ink offset also depends on the machine's fonts.
Options: (a) measure the padding, not the ink: assert the selection-row inset, or the pad value in the ini (my preference; pad 12 then stays, row inset is 6 / 6); (b) chora-xp --run-box picks the pad by output scale (13 at 1x, 12 at 2x) and passes `--horizontal-pad`; needs the output scale in chora-xp (not available there today); (c) accept a 1 px tolerance on the ink. Current head: 1x red (11), 2x green (12).
