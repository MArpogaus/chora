## 1. The five questions (priorities-sprint1 section 4)

- **MAJOR, plan 1.2 (plan:71-79): the hosted cube makes Erika depend on construct, and the plan does not define "no full step out" or test it.** The owner said (answers.md:380, 10:35): "Turn cube in xp without full breakout to construct still possible. Like before: switching desktops, turning it with mouse." "Hosted" means the xp flavor always runs on construct's virtual outputs. D69 (decisions.md:50) says such an output "ends with its creator". So a construct crash removes the desktop Erika is looking at, and direct scanout is lost at rest, which the plan calls an unmeasured risk. The W5 acts (plan:306) check "turn by key, by pager, by drag" but never check that the session ends back at 1:1 with no construct HUD and no navigate mode. Change: (1) make the lazy cube the default for the xp flavor (desks bind to virtual outputs only while turning, plan:78-79); keep hosting for the construct flavor. (2) Add an act contract: after a turn started from xp, construct is stepped in at 1:1, no construct surface is visible, and the xp taskbar has the focus. (3) The plan says nothing about the case where construct dies mid-turn; add it.
- **MAJOR, plan 1.2/1.3 (plan:68-69, 83-86): "desk j = face j" only works for one look on all faces.** D4 lets each face run its own manager, possibly another look. Carrying a window to a face of another look is then a give plus a turn, and the plan names no owner for a window while `cube.drag` crosses that boundary. That is the co-18 lost-window class again. The claim "this gives D4 without a cube change" is not shown. Change: specify drag-across-looks as `give` + `cube.turn`, with I1 held across the boundary. Name tiling and mc as cube drivers (DB12: "navigable from every look", backlog.md:178), or state their honest limit. Today only xp drives the cube in W5.
- **MAJOR, plan 1.4 (plan:97-105), record 9: the Fluent decision reverses the owner's newest choice and also departs from his C12 wording.** At 04:38 the owner picked "Extend kit tables" (answers.md:373, D92 "No Fluent", decisions.md:135). That came after C12 (11-16, backlog.md:171). The plan presents the choice as "owner vs ARC" (plan:97). Even its Fluent variant drops three things C12 asked for: i18n-embed, the "runtime switch over IPC", and "voice models and prompts per language". `include_str!` compiles the .ftl files into each plugin, which contradicts the plan's own reason "new languages as files without code" (plan:98). Change: file only step 1 (typed keys, the same under either store). Put the store choice to the owner as two options: tables, or Fluent loaded at run time from installed files with an IPC switch. The PM cannot overturn an owner pick of 04:38.
- **MAJOR, plan 1.4 (plan:90-96), record 7: "no second registry" is not true.** Voice already has a user-configurable per-tool `confirm` list (services/voice/src/config.rs:273, chat.rs:1124). A new per-export `risk: ask` would become a second source of truth for "ask first". design/grants.md separates the grant (may it happen at all) from confirm (ask every time) (grants.md:58-59). It also answers the grant question with physical keys including `always` (grants.md:112-120), which `kit::ask` (A4) does not model. C0's tool `Source` registry, i.e. external tool plugins as sandboxed MCP servers (backlog.md:77, needed by playbook beat 1), is not a method of the core table at all. Change: state which one wins between `risk` and voice `confirm`, add `always` and grant questions to `kit::ask`, and give external Sources a row in the registry design.
- **MINOR, plan 1.5 (plan:109-111): "safe now" items have no lane.** These are mc-32 verify, P1, item 19 and the DB14 cell. mc-32 is a BLOCK (findings/mc.md:22). Change: give mc-32 verify a haiku lane in the week (S, no architecture need).
