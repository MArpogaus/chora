# f25 r2: the cause of the doubled glow, fixed (claude/f25 at df101c1f)

## Cause
`MeshElement::draw` (crates/chora/src/mesh.rs) ignored the `damage` it gets from the
damage tracker and drew its whole quad. The tracker clears only the damage (minus opaque
regions) and draws each element into it, so a translucent mesh element (the glow `tf-*` and
shadow `ts-*` shader nodes of chora-tiling, opacity 0.5/0.25) blended a second time over the
frame before in every part outside the damage. A frame whose damage is only the window
rectangles (foot repainting after a layout change) doubled the ring of glow around them.
Whether the last frame before step 6 is such a frame is timing: 3 of 6 CI runs red.

## Evidence
- Reproduced in the CI image (docker, registry.fedoraproject.org/fedora:44@sha256:52740d9b,
  the dnf list of the e2e job, `cargo build --workspace`, headless sway 1.11): 8 of 10 runs of
  tests/agent.sh steps 1-6 red with exactly "pixel 5 5: 10 8 15 255 ... host shows 19 15 28".
  Ubuntu sway 1.9 here: 0 of 95 red (release and debug, idle and loaded): the host compositor
  version does not matter, chora's own frame does.
- Full-frame diff (run red): 33008 pixels differ, only the glow ring (screen border 0-14 px and
  the gap between the windows); the host is 1.7-1.9x the capture there, windows identical.
- Frame log (CHORA_DBG): age=1 on every frame after the first; the frames before step 6 have
  damage [(14,14,619,772),(647,14,619,772)] only, glow elements lie outside it.
- Isolation: forcing buffer age 0 (full damage): 10/10 green. Submitting full damage to the
  host with the real age: still red, so chora's framebuffer is wrong, not the host or sway.
- Unit red: `render::damage::a_translucent_shader_blends_once_when_only_a_corner_of_it_changes`
  (commit cfb4e26): a half-red veil over black reads 192, the first frame read 128.

## Fix (df101c1f)
`gl::draw_within` scissors the draw to each rectangle of the damage (through
`gl::backdrop::window_rect`, as the glass does) and gives the scissor back;
`MeshElement::draw` uses it. No tolerance or test change.

## Green
- unit test green; `cargo test -p chora --lib` in the Fedora image: 588 passed.
- step 6 loop in the CI image, debug build: 30 of 30 green (was 8 of 10 red).
- tests/merge-gate.sh --jobs 3 once: all steps passed (incl. e2e, 2d-guard, tiling-audit,
  doors; log dir /tmp/merge-gate.uPfNRw).
