### Cause

Two causes, one in xp and one in the core:

1. xp, at its start (`plugins/chora-xp/src/wm/startup.rs` `adopt_all`), with no window, gave its desktop icons the keyboard (`focus(None)`, then `desktop_keys`) even when another look held a claim. Rule 3 of docs/api.md "Window managers" then gave the window of tiling's Super+Return to xp. `node_focused` in state.rs was not the cause: it only gives back icon keys that a foreign window holds.
2. With xp leaving the keyboard alone, nothing has the keyboard and the pointer starts at 0,0, outside both claims (tiling [0,40,1280,730] and xp [0,40,1280,760] headless; [0,80,..] in the persona). `Scene::manager_of_new` then reserved the window for nobody, so either look could attach it.

### Fix (rule D119, docs/api.md rule 5 and the join paragraph)

- core: rule 5, `State::first_manager` (crates/chora/src/state.rs) gives `NewToplevel.first` (crates/chora-scene/src/scene.rs). With no parent, no choice, no focus and the pointer in no claim, a new window goes to the live window manager with a claim whose connection is the oldest.
- xp: `Arrival::Joined` (plugins/chora-xp/src/wm.rs). xp joined beside another look's claim and has no window, so it leaves the keyboard where it is. `state.get` says `holder: null`; `focus_moved` gives the icons no keys when a window of the other look closes. A wallpaper click in a smaller claim of the other look (`press_wallpaper`, the persona click) takes no keys, because the core chose that look for the click. A click on an icon, a window of xp that gets the focus, or a node of xp that takes the keys (Start menu) ends `Joined`.
- tests/gate-handover-shared.sh expected xp to take the keyboard when it joined. It now checks `holder` none after the join and clicks a desktop icon of xp before its foot.
