Keyboard shortcuts

3 keys contain “cube”. Press Esc to show all keys.

The cube

Ctrl+Alt+Left, RightTurn the cube to the next face
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+LeftCarry the window round the cube
Super+drag desktopTurn the cube by hand

All other keys

Ctrl+EscOpen the Start menu
Alt+TabSwitch to the next window
Alt+F4Close the window
Super+EOpen a terminal
Shift+F10Open the menu of the item
Super+AOpen the chat
Alt+Y, Alt+NAnswer yes or no to a question
Super+Alt+1 to 4Give the window to another look
EscBack out one step
Super+/ or F1Show this window
