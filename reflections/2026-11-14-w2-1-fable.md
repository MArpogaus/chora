## 3. Autonomy

### Why the owner steered so much

1. No visible plan. The owner wrote the strategy himself (judge 11-12 point 1: 07:04, 07:06, 10:30, 16:47). state.md had no RC burn-down, and still has none.
2. The owner saw flaws first: paperclip, tiling "ugly", user tile, tray, mc bar (friction 28; decisions-now.md 104). Reviews judged notes, not pixels or function.
3. The PM obeyed instead of weighing. Every idea turned into a rule, a file or a lane in the same turn (P1). That teaches the owner that steering works.
4. The PM asked or stalled on reversible matters; "decide on your own" was said twice (judge 11-12 point 1).
5. The owner contributed. He declared "no more intervention" and sent nine messages after it. He changed two product goals on day 5 at 93 % budget. He set "perfection" as the bar. He posted two meter readings one minute apart (69 % and 96 %). A PM with a spine would have named each of these at once.

### What the PM decides alone

Everything reversible: taste, design defaults, lane order, models, rounds, which finding is fixed first, what goes to known issues, rules and routines, test methods, merge timing, cutting its own planned work.

### What goes to the owner

- A change of a goal on the vision card or of an RC criterion, and an RC slip of more than two days.
- Cutting a look or a promised capability.
- Legal and licence matters (Microsoft assets).
- Outward acts: pull request, tag, publish, branch deletion, force push.
- Environment, permissions and budget resets.
- Approval of each look (rc 1b) and the hardware smoke.

Batched under "Needs owner" in state.md, at most once a day, each with a default and a deadline. No answer by the deadline means the default holds.

### How to handle a new idea from the owner

1. Classify it in one line, in the reply: READING, QUESTION, BUG, IDEA, GOAL CHANGE, ORDER.
2. BUG: one line in the look's findings ledger with a severity. It waits its turn by severity.
3. IDEA: one line in backlog.md with an id. Reply: "Logged as N7. After the RC. Reason: ...". No lane changes.
4. GOAL CHANGE: reply with the cost first: what slips, by how long, what is dropped. Apply only after the owner confirms, and only at the next lane start (guard P1).
5. ORDER (the owner says "now"): do it, and say what it displaces.
6. A conflict with a standing decision or the RC: say so in the same reply, with the line it conflicts with. Silence is not agreement.

The reply stays two lines. The PM never rewrites a rule in the turn the idea arrives.
