    chora-mc style guide¶

    These rules apply to every page of chora-mc: the built-in scenes, new scenes and new kinds of content. The model is a calm book page: few sizes, one weight, a clear hierarchy and the same space everywhere.

    This page gives the rules. plugins/chora-mc/mc.yaml holds the values: the styles, the colors, the scale and the page. When a value changes, change it there, not here.

    Type¶

    1. Two families. TeX Gyre Pagella sets the text that people read: the scene title, the panel titles, the body and the lead. Inter sets the frame and machine data: the header, the footer, the sides, the margin notes, the HUD and the style data. The style mono is for commands, paths and code only. Do not add a third family.

    2. Five sizes. Use only these steps of the scale:

      Step Role Styles
      -1 margin notes, regions, HUD, code margin, hud, mono
      0 body text and data body, data
      1 panel titles heading
      2 the lead of a scene, at most one lead
      3 the scene title and its number chapter, chapter_number

      Do not set Pagella below step 0. The labels of the regions are the one exception: capitals of Inter at 0.61 em, as tall as the lowercase of margin. 3. One weight. Pagella is always regular. Inter is regular, the labels of the regions are medium (500). Do not use bold or italic for emphasis; use place, size and space. 4. Case and letter spacing. The scene title is in capitals with 0.12 em. Panel titles are in small caps (smcp and c2sc) with 0.06 em. The labels of the regions are capitals of Inter with 0.2 em. Lowercase text has no letter spacing. 5. Figures. Body text has old-style figures (onum). The scene title and its number have lining figures (lnum), so that the number stands as tall as the capitals. Times in the margin and the clock have tabular figures (tnum). The style data has no tnum, because Inter then sets hyphens wide.

    Color¶

    1. Three roles. text is for content. muted is for panel titles, labels, notes, the scene number and its bar. faint is for hairlines and for an agent that rests. Do not set text that people must read in faint.
    2. One accent. The accent color is on the scene title and on the state of the voice agent only. The colors green, amber, blue and red show a state, for example of an agent or an error; do not use them for decoration.

    Space¶

    1. Baseline grid. Each line of text is a whole number of lines of the grid (scale.baseline). Give space in lines of the grid, not in pixels. A panel title has one blank line under it. The scene head has one blank line under it.
    2. One left edge. The scene number, the panel titles and the body start at the left edge of their text column. Only margin notes, such as times, go into the margin column, flush right.
    3. The scene head. The number, the bar and the title stand on one baseline. The bar is a hairline from the baseline to the height of the capitals, with the same space on both sides.
    4. Hairlines, no boxes. A rule is one pixel in faint, left of a panel. There is no rule at the left edge of the text block and no rule beside an empty panel. Do not add boxes, fills or shadows.

    Layout¶

    1. Titles never clip. A panel title has one to three words. A longer title wraps and moves the text of its panel down. A panel shows whole lines only; it never cuts through a line.
    2. Narrow outputs. 1280 logical pixels is a main target, not an edge case. A page shows only as many columns as fit at min_panel; the other panels wrap into new rows in reading order. A row is never lower than min_panel. When the page is longer than the output, it scrolls with the wheel.
    3. Columns follow the content. The agents scene has as many columns as agents: one agent takes the whole width, two take half each. A new scene with a varying number of items does the same.

    New scenes and contents¶

    1. Use the named styles. A scene or a content uses body, heading, lead, data, margin or mono. It changes a style only with base and a color or a step of the table above. Do not set a family, a size in pixels or a letter spacing in a scene file. A new role gets a named style in mc.yaml and a rule on this page. Before a merge, capture the scene at 1920x1080 and at 1280x720, in the dark and the light theme, and compare it with these rules.
