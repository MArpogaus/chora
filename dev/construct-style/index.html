    chora-construct style guide¶

    These rules apply to every element that chora-construct draws over its space: the voice HUD, the window list, the pane labels and the confirmation questions. The space is a calm 3D void. The light theme has a white glossy floor, a crisp horizon and a pale sky. The dark theme has a black void over a black glossy floor. Panes float with a fixed turn; walking past one shows it at an angle. All elements share one visual language: floating glass, few sizes, one accent and calm motion.

    This page gives the rules. The section style of plugins/chora-construct/construct.yaml holds the values, as mc.yaml does for chora-mc. The tables below show the defaults of that section. When a value changes, change it in the file and run the contrast test of rule 5. plans/construct-hud.md adds the section.

    Type¶

    1. Two families. Inter sets labels and text. DejaVu Sans Mono sets data: commands, paths, owners, sizes and the detail of a question. Do not add a third family.

    2. Three sizes. Use only these sizes, in logical pixels:

      Role Size Line Family and face
      label 11 16 Inter 500, capitals, letter spacing 0.16 em
      data 12 16 DejaVu Sans Mono 400
      text 16 24 Inter 400

      A label names a thing or a state, for example WINDOWS or LISTENING. Text is what people read: a spoken sentence, a prompt, a window name. Do not use bold, italic or other sizes.

    Colour¶

    1. Four roles per theme. glass fills a panel. edge is the hairline of a panel. text is for content and the selected row. muted is for labels, notes and rows that are not selected.

      Role Light Dark
      glass [1.00, 0.99, 0.975, 0.94], a warm white [0.04, 0.05, 0.07, 0.86]
      edge [0.08, 0.09, 0.12, 0.24] [1.00, 1.00, 1.00, 0.16]
      text [0.08, 0.09, 0.12] (#14181E) [0.93, 0.93, 0.95] (#ECEEF2)
      muted [0.29, 0.32, 0.38] (#4A5260) [0.64, 0.67, 0.71] (#A3AAB5)
      glow accent at 0.10 accent at 0.22

    2. One accent for both themes. accent is [0.15, 0.50, 0.71] (#2680B4). It colours the state ring, the waveform, the selection mark, the glow and the edge of the focused pane. It never colours text. Do not use other hues.

    3. Contrast. Check each colour on the glass over the worst background: a black pane and a white pane. The minimum contrast ratios of the defaults are:

      Role Light Dark Threshold
      text 15.4 11.6 7.0
      muted 6.8 5.7 4.5
      accent 3.8 3.1 3.0 (graphics only)

      On the bare floor and the sky, accent reaches 3.8 or more in both themes. A unit test computes these ratios from construct.yaml and fails below a threshold.

    Glass¶

    1. The glass panel. A panel is a rect with the glass fill, a corner radius of 12 and a hairline of 1 in edge. A glow in accent surrounds it and falls to zero within 16. Panes behind the panel do not blur. A node texture of the pane group renders the pane shaders without their node textures, so a blur shows empty panes. The opacity keeps the text readable instead: 0.94 in the light theme, so that the glass over a black pane stays a clean warm white (#F0EDEA) and not a flat grey, and 0.86 in the dark theme.
    2. The glow is the only shader. The rect carries the fill and the hairline as core props. A shader node above it draws only the glow, the state ring and the waveform. When the watchdog removes the shaders, the rect and the text stay readable. A pane label has no glow.

    Space and place¶

    1. One spacing unit of 8. Give every padding, gap, line and margin as a whole number of units. A panel has a padding of 16. A row of a list is 24 high. A screen-fixed element keeps 32 from the edge of the output.
    2. Screen-fixed on screen:OUTPUT. The voice HUD, the window list and the questions are flat nodes under the group of the space on the screen root. They never turn or scale with the camera. The stack from the back is: panes, pane labels, voice HUD, window list, question.
    3. Labels sit in the space, drawn flat. A pane label sits 8 below the projected bottom edge of its pane, centred. It is a flat node at that point, not a projected one, so it stays sharp. Only the pane ahead and the pane under the pointer show a label.
    4. Text only on glass. Text never sits on the bare space or on a pane. Every text stays inside its panel, within the padding, as the core measures it. A spoken sentence wraps at 480, or narrower on a small output, and shows at most two lines. Longer text keeps its end and starts with an ellipsis. A question wraps, and its panel grows with it. A text that must stay on one line, such as a row of the window list or a pane label (at most 320 wide), ends with an ellipsis.

    Motion¶

    1. Calm and short. An element fades in over 160 ms, with its glow rising over 240 ms. It fades out over 120 ms. All motion eases out. Use the core animate for fades, not frames of the plugin.

      Motion Timing
      the arc of thinking one turn in 1.2 s
      the arc of working one turn in 2.4 s
      the pulse of question opacity 0.6 to 1.0, a period of 2 s
      the bars of speaking for the duration_ms of the sentence
      a spoken sentence stays 2 s after its end, then fades out

      Do not bounce, scale, slide or flash.

    Contents¶

    1. What each element shows.

      Element Contents
      voice HUD the state ring; the state as a label; while speaking, the sentence; while thinking or working, the task in muted
      question the label QUESTION; the prompt in text; the whole detail in data; the label ANSWER YES OR NO
      window list the label WINDOWS or GIVE TO; per row, the name in text and a muted note in data; an accent mark left of the selected row
      pane label the name of the pane as a label, in text, on a glass pill without glow

    2. The state never depends on colour or motion alone. The label always names the state. Then the HUD stays clear without shaders and for people who do not see the accent.

    3. New elements. A new element uses the glass panel, the three sizes and the four roles. A new role gets a value in the section style and a rule on this page. Before a merge, capture it at 1920x1080 and 1280x720, in both themes, over a white and a black pane, and compare it with these rules.

    Panes¶

    1. The focused pane. The focused pane has full opacity and an accent hairline with a soft glow. It is the pane flown into, else the pane under the pointer or ahead, as the pane label picks it. The other panes fade into the fog with their distance.
