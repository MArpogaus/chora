# Handover: core death, memory, watchdogs (branch claude/core-death, HEAD c3861bc)
Build with `CARGO_PROFILE_DEV_DEBUG=0 cargo build --workspace` (target 2.2 GB instead of about 7 GB). Scripts and raw data: /tmp/claude-0/-home-user/8a28ab73-2182-54dd-8448-69a0fbb58e8a/scratchpad (sample.sh, leak9.sh, leak11.sh, starve.sh, *.rss, *.run).
## Found, with evidence
- No core died in W9, W11, W12, alone or all three beside 6 busy loops (exit 0 each; the status of the core is in /tmp/bt-WF.env.err).
- Hang watchdog (D64) aborted a healthy core: the time its own thread did not run (SIGSTOP, starved host) counted as a stall. Red unit test: `Some(Abort(39.75s))` after a 40 s gap. Under 8 parallel sessions the log shows "the main loop stalls ... (stalled 3 s)" in the 300 panel scene and the 10000 node transaction, so 30 s under a swap storm is plausible.
- SIGTERM, SIGINT, SIGHUP ended the core with no line; the quit binding and the nested window close logged nothing. No panic, SIGPIPE or OOM kill seen (Rust ignores SIGPIPE; SIGKILL can leave nothing, exit 137).
- Memory: a high-water mark, not a leak. Ten repeats of each phase reach a plateau (MB): windows 115>135, layouts 115>133 (peaks 174), hotplug 132, restart 130, capture 115>161 (moves 157..172), mc switch 135, panel 127, 300 panels 137, apps 131..139, 10000 nodes 127>161>174>177 flat. Whole runs: W9 118>156 (peak 282 under load), W11 122>219, W12 137>247 (the 10000 creates and 5000 nested groups). The 955 MB of 10-30 did not reproduce.
- mc and the frame watchdog: the headless budget is one refresh of wall time. Alone, the 300 panel scene gives 0 watchdog lines. With 8 sessions on 4 cores: "blames a client client=mc load=16542", 4.5 s later "disconnects a client client=mc", and "drops all effects load=326" 10 times for plain scene switches. Cause: the watchdog cannot tell a starved core from a costly scene. chora-mc also does not handle `effects.dropped` (tiling, xp, construct do) and keeps the write effect on every panel after the animation (load 16542 for 300 panels).
